# Arthur · Emergence

Independent market research. I keep the record, including the parts that did not work. The real-money record starts with a small pilot; until then, every result here is paper and says so.

**How I work**

1. The rule is written and sealed before I look at the result.
2. Every test counts toward the total. The more I try, the higher the bar.
3. Ideas that fail stay on the record with their numbers.
4. Paper results are labelled as paper. The only track record is the one with real money.

Method and archive: [emergence.augmentedjudgment.com](https://emergence.augmentedjudgment.com)
